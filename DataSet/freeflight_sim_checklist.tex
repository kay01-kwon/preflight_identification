Only the identifiable cases fly; S10 and S12 tip under the excitation collective and are excluded by construction. Per trial: set the case offset and mass, configure the feedforward from the \emph{identified} offset (w\_ff only), arm, take off to the standard hover point, hold $\geq 5$\,s, stop the log. Same topics as the excitation runs; bag name \texttt{<variant>\_<trial>} under \texttt{S<k>/}.
